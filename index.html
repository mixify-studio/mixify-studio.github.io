Our Crafting Philosophy

What makes a Mixify Studio app special? We design each product from the ground up to achieve three key values.

Visual Excellence

Cute, pastel-colored aesthetic styles tailored to perfection. We believe that screen interfaces should be highly visual, artistic, and pleasing to look at.

Playful Interactions

Interactive mechanics that are simple to use yet full of fun, like our silly interactive wallpaper touch effects and step-by-step drawing guidance.

Made with Love

We build each app with care, ensuring robust updates, secure and clean children-friendly code, and supportive customer care to keep you smiling.

Frequently Asked Questions

Got questions about our services or app download setups? We have answers.

How do I download Mixify Studio apps?

All of our official applications are distributed through the Google Play Store. Simply click "Download" on our showcase cards or visit our Google Play Developer profile directly to download safely.

Are the applications free to use?

Yes, our basic versions are free to download and use. We support our free apps through unintrusive ads (AdMob). Some apps offer optional premium subscriptions for ad-free usage and exclusive content.

Can I use the wallpapers for commercial projects?

All aesthetic wallpapers and artwork inside our apps are strictly for personal, non-commercial use on personal mobile devices. Redistributing, printing for sale, or selling them is prohibited.

Who can I contact for support or collaboration?

We welcome your feedback and support queries! You can contact us directly by email at info.mixify.studio@gmail.com or use the contact form below.
